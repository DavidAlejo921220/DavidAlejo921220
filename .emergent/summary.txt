<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to develop a marketplace web application, similar to InDriver, but focused on on-demand tow truck services.

The platform must have three distinct front-end interfaces:
1.  **Client Frontend**:
    *   Registration/Login with OTP verification (SMS or email).
    *   A service request form including vehicle type, make/model, condition, pickup/destination points on an interactive map, and photo uploads.
    *   A bidding model where the client posts a request, drivers send price offers, and the client can accept or reject them.
    *   Service status tracking (e.g., Created, In Negotiation, Driver en route, Finished).
    *   Real-time chat with the driver, including location sharing.
    *   Service history and driver ratings.

2.  **Driver Frontend**:
    *   Registration with personal data, documents, and tow truck details.
    *   A real-time list of nearby service requests with map view and filtering.
    *   Ability to send and negotiate price offers.
    *   Service management (updating status, integrated navigation).
    *   Photo uploads for evidence at pickup and drop-off.
    *   Chat with the client, service history, and accumulated earnings view.

3.  **Admin Panel (Backoffice)**:
    *   Dashboard with key metrics (services per day, revenue, active drivers, etc.).
    *   Management of users, drivers, and services, including suspension/blocking.
    *   Reputation and cancellation history management.
    *   Dynamic configuration of platform commissions.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
*   **Commission System**: Configurable commission (percentage or fixed), can vary by vehicle type or zone.
*   **Reputation System**: Bidirectional ratings (client rates driver, driver rates client).
*   **Anti-Fraud**: OTP verification, document validation, and suspicious behavior tracking.
*   **Geolocation**: Definition of active zones, automatic distance calculation.
*   **Business Model**: Primarily a bidding marketplace, with optional modes for system-suggested pricing or automatic Uber-style assignment.
*   **Technical Stack**: REST API, WebSockets for real-time features, and a relational database (PostgreSQL or MySQL).
